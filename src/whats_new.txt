## 2.8.5
- Racing wheels: the game gives a thumbstick and the triggers a 20% dead zone. The wheel now makes up for it, so small turns and light pedal presses count (issue #53: it only steered near full lock).
- Racing wheels: a pedal that reads fully pressed with your foot off it is reversed by itself, which should fix a car that stands still and vibrates on throttle. The log says when it does this.
- Racing wheels: wheel buttons 1 to 10 press A, B, X, Y, Black, White, Start, Back and the stick clicks, and the hat is the d-pad. The log names each button you press. Choose your own with --set RECOMP_WHEEL_BUTTONS=A:3,B:1.
- Racing wheels: with Feedback above 0, the wheel gets a centring spring while you drive.

## 2.8.4
- Fixed: far-away trees floating in the haze after the ground had faded into fog (issue #50). Anything past the far plane is now fogged like the rest of the scene.
- Fixed: Steam Game Recording said "Recording other game" (issue #47). The game no longer opens a hidden extra window or a console window next to itself. With Vulkan ticked, point the Steam shortcut at mm3_recomp_vulkan.exe.
- Racing wheels that Windows lists as a plain DirectInput device (Saitek, some Thrustmaster and Logitech models) are now found too (issue #53). The log lists every joystick it looked at: tick "Detailed log" if your wheel is still missed.
- New Gameplay mod: Opponents use full-detail cars (issue #51), off by default.

## 2.8.3
- Fixed: menus skipped entries with a controller in the DirectX 11 renderer. A controller on a later XInput slot (Steam Input, a re-plugged pad) was also read as player 1, so every press counted twice.
- New in the launcher: "Install an older version" lists the earlier releases, downloads the one you pick and opens its Setup.
- The launcher option "Detailed stutter log" is now "Detailed log (for bug reports)". It also logs every missing game file, controller input and camera cuts.

## 2.8.2
- Fixed: the loading screen was black with only the LOADING animation when you drove one of the 14 downloadable (DLC) cars. They now show a loading picture.
- Fixed: with the Steam overlay, the loading screen after quitting a race could be black with only the LOADING animation, or cut off and stretched in a corner.
- Faster loading: going back to the menu after a race and loading the menus take about a second less.
- Fixed: trees and street props vanishing while driving, mostly at night.
- New option: Unlock all races and jobs (Gameplay mods). Every installed Checkpoint race and Undercover job is open; your save is not changed.
- New option: Digital speedometer, the speed number from split screen, in normal play too.
- Steam: a shortcut to "Play Midtown Madness 3.cmd" now gets the Steam overlay, FPS counter and game recording. Setup also gives the Steam shortcut its cover, banner, logo and icon.
- Steam: the empty console window that opened next to the game is gone.
- Smoother pacing with VSync on when the frame limit is above your screen's refresh rate.
- Graphics: the slider shows "Custom" when your settings match no level, the Max level runs at 120 FPS, and a fresh install starts on the Xbox level.
- Graphics: the internal resolution list is in order and named by height (720p, 1080p, 1440p, 4K) for your aspect ratio, in the launcher and in F10.
- Many fixes in the F10 Display Settings window and the launcher, for DirectX 11 and Vulkan.
- Launcher: new "Detailed stutter log" option for bug reports. Press F9 in game to save the last seconds of frames next to the log.
- This window: after each update it lists what is new.
